My Favourite Hobby: Photography

Photography is one of my favourite hobbies because it allows me to capture moments in time. Anyone can start with a simple camera or even a phone.

Why I Love Photography

  • It helps me notice detail in the world
  • I enjoy creativity
  • It allows me to share memories

Steps to Take a Good Photo

  1. Find an interesting subject
  2. Make sure there is good lighting
  3. Frame the subject carefully
  4. Take multiple shots
